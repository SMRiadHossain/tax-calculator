This website helps salaried employees in Bangladesh understand their tax structure, exemptions, and plan investments to minimize tax. You can also use the interactive tax calculator below.

Allowances & Tax Exemptions

Allowance Type Max Exempt Amount / Notes
House Rent 50% of basic salary is exempt (city corp). Enter actual house rent received.

Conveyance Max 30,000 BDT/year exempt. Enter actual conveyance received.

Provident Fund Employee 100% contribution, Employer ≤10% basic exempt. Enter amount contributed.
Insurance Premium Actual premium paid is exempt if approved.

Investments & Tax Rebate

You can reduce your tax by investing in approved instruments. A 15% rebate applies to eligible investments such as:

  • Bank Deposit Schemes (DPS)
  • Stock market (approved stocks or mutual funds)
  • Life Insurance Premiums
  • Government-approved mutual funds

Tips to Minimize Tax

  • Invest in approved instruments to get a 15% rebate.
  • Ensure HRA, medical, and conveyance are properly documented in salary slip.
  • Consider provident fund contributions.
  • Check company festival bonus and insurance schemes for exemptions.
  • Use the calculator to find the minimum investment required to reduce tax to the DNCC minimum.
