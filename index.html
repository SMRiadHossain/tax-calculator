Standard Exemption (Section 32)

Under Finance Ordinance 2025, no individual per-component exemptions (HRA, Medical, Conveyance) exist. A single consolidated exemption applies: the lesser of 1/3 of Gross Annual Salary or BDT 500,000.

Investments & Tax Rebate Section 78

A flat 15% rebate applies to eligible investments. The rebate principal is capped at the lowest of:

  • Actual eligible investments (DPS capped at BDT 120,000)
  • 20% of your Net Taxable Income
  • BDT 1,000,000

Tips to Minimize Tax

  • Maximize DPS: Up to BDT 120,000 in DPS counts toward the rebate. At 15%, that's up to BDT 18,000 saved.
  • Buy Sanchayapatra: Government savings bonds are among the safest eligible investments with competitive yields.
  • File on time: Submit your return by November 30 — late filing forfeits the entire 15% investment rebate.
  • Understand the standard exemption: The 1/3 rule automatically applies. No need to split your salary into separate allowances for tax purposes.
  • Check perquisite impact: A company car adds BDT 180,000–600,000 to your taxable income. Factor this in when evaluating compensation packages.
  • Use the calculator's optimization tip to find the exact additional investment needed to minimize your tax to the statutory minimum.
